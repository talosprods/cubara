## 1. What the issue asks, and what binds

Goal: *"A detail swap is never a one-frame change. It slides over about 250 ms of wall time,
with no hole on any frame and no cost on the static gate."*

Binding decisions, quoted from the issue body:

1. *"Sliding cross-fade, not a dither. When a swap happens (in `crates/render/src/uploads.rs`,
   at the point the Remove of the outgoing nodes would apply after their replacements
   uploaded), both are drawn for `FADE` = 250 ms: First half: the incoming nodes start Δ low
   and rise to their place. Second half: the outgoing nodes sink by Δ and are then removed."*
2. *"Δ is one vertical cell of the coarser of the two levels: 1 block at levels 1–2, 4 at
   level 3 (`cell_height_blocks`). Only `node_origins[i].y` changes. There is no shader
   change and no discard … A bias of 0.01 block avoids z-fighting at the hand-over."*
3. *"Edits never fade. An Insert that replaces an existing id (`NodeStreaming::invalidate`)
   swaps at once, as today. Only batches that carry Removes fade, and only their new ids
   rise."* — made exact by amendment A.
4. *"Time is passed in, not read (Rule 2) … The fade's offset is a pure function of
   (elapsed, Δ, role) with unit tests."*
5. *"… a node that must leave mid-rise sinks from where it is, and a returning id cancels its
   sink and returns to full height. **At every instant, over every footprint, at least one of
   old or new is at full height (no hole, no crack).** This is the property test."* — made
   exact by amendment B.
6. *"Cost: nothing on the static gate (no swap, no writes). During a swap, only the fading
   nodes' origins are written (a few vec4 per frame) … Measure `--bench gate` before/after
   (within noise) and `--bench flight` p99/p99.9/max and holes (must stay 0)."*

F14 amendment (issue body, 2026-11-10):

7. *"Done-when is the pop metric and the owner's eye — not gate item 0's hole or
   stale-terrain terms."* The pop metric: *"largest single-frame pixel change in the swapped
   area … must drop by at least 4× with the fade, and the total change over the fade must stay
   the same (the same final image)."* Holes: must not regress. Stale: *"explicitly not
   expected to move, and the PR says so with the number before and after."*
8. *"Measure the residual pop distribution per level first … The table goes in this issue as
   a comment and in the PR … If the residual at levels 0↔1 and 1↔2 is routinely > Δ,
   decision 2 is revisited here, with the numbers, before implementation — not after."*
9. The property test must include a node that is at once a rising child and a sinking parent,
   and a node whose id returns mid-sink.

**S2** is not a comment on #380 (the issue has none). It is review finding S2 of #379's

## 3. Approach, reasoning, decisions

### The mechanism

Per node one number, a **lift** ≤ 0 in blocks, added to `node_origins[i].y`; kept in
`NodeSlot`; formed by one arena helper used by `insert`, `rebase` and `set_lift`. A pure
state machine `Fades` (`crates/render/src/fade.rs`, no wgpu) is advanced once per frame with
the caller's `frame_dt`. With `H = FADE / 2 = 125 ms`:

- **Incoming** (`rise = Δ > 0`, id not in the arena): placed at `−Δ`, rises at `Δ / H` to 0.
- **Outgoing** (`sink = Δ > 0`, Remove ready): stays; lift drops to `−BIAS` (0.01) at once;
  **holds for H**; sinks at `Δ / H` to `−Δ`; then the real `arena.remove`.
- `rise == 0`, `sink == 0`, or fade 0: exactly today's behaviour.

No-hole argument (re-derived by the review): a Remove is ready only after its replacements
are uploaded; a rise takes at most H; the hold is H. So when a node starts to sink, each
replacement is at full height or has handed over to *its* replacements by the same rule.

### A and B — engineering readings (confirmed by the review)

**A (decision 3, per node).** The caller states per node whether it is part of a swap and
the swap's Δ: `rise` on an arriving mesh, `sink` on a leaving id. An Insert for an id that is
resident and not fading never fades (an edit), whatever `rise` says.

**B (decision 5).** A node whose Remove becomes ready mid-rise **keeps rising through its
hold** (capped at `−BIAS`), then sinks from where it is, which is full height. Freezing
breaks the property: O replaced by N (N rising, O holding until `t_O + H`); N's children N′
land at `t_N`, `t_O < t_N < t_O + H`; a frozen N leaves `[t_O + H, t_N + H)` with O sinking,
N low and N′ still rising. Side effect to note in the docs: in a three-level cascade inside
125 ms, N capped at `−BIAS` is coplanar with its own held parent for a few ms.

### C–F — lead decisions, proposed

**C. Trees — lead decision, proposed.** *An incoming node's tree instances are inserted when
its rise completes; an outgoing node's instances are removed when its hold ends.* Both
moments have old and new at full height, so identical oaks hand over invisibly, as today.
Reasoning: the owner's goal is "as little visible as possible"; a plain slide makes a
hand-over that is invisible today visible on every tree. The same rule is used at 2↔3 (oak ↔
box, visible today too) for one rule rather than two. **Level-0 voxel trees** are part of the
chunk mesh and cannot be separated without a shader change (decision 2 excludes it): at the
ring-0 edge they still show a Δ band for one frame; it is measured and reported, not worked
around. Alternative rejected: slide instances with the node (the regression described in §2).

**D. The pop measure — lead decision, proposed.** One deciding number, fixed in advance:
- Setup: camera fixed at the low flight's start `(8,120,8)` looking `(1,−0.15,0.3)`; the
  **streaming** eye moved +16 blocks in x, eight times, each step drawn until settled.
- Frame step **fixed at 1/60 s** (the flights' display rate; never shorter, never changed to
  pass) and size **1920×1080** (what gate item 0 is judged at).
- **Measure: `changed_pixels(.., POP_TOLERANCE)` between consecutive frames, inside the
  union of the screen boxes of every node `swapped_out()` reported during that step** (the
  same union for both arms — built from both arms' runs), maximised over all frames of the
  eight steps.
- **Pass: median over three runs of the fade-on maximum × 4 ≤ median of the fade-off
  maximum.** All six maxima go in the PR. Final frame of each step byte-equal on vs off (any
  difference reported with its pixel count).
- Diagnostics only, never deciding: land↔nothing flips, `silhouette_shift`, and the
  land→land share of the maximum frame.
- Why immune to step and pacing: `frame_dt` is a constant handed to the renderer, not the
  wall clock, so a fade always spans 15 frames; the area is the swapped nodes only, so far
  terrain and new edge nodes do not dilute or inflate it. Mesh arrival still depends on the
  worker threads — that is what three runs and the median are for (median of three: chosen,
  not derived).
- **If the flips drop ≥ 4× and the deciding measure does not** (the coplanar texture/AO
  switch of §2): proposed — PR 2b **merges as an improvement only if nothing got worse**
  (fade-on maximum ≤ fade-off maximum on the deciding measure, HOLES and STALE per F), with
  both numbers and the land→land share in the PR; #380 **stays open**, not "done"; the owner's
  eye decides whether a texture cross-fade (a shader change) gets its own issue. Reasoning:
  the silhouette slide is strictly less visible than a pop, holding it back serves nobody,
  but F14's done-when is not met and must not be claimed. If the deciding measure got *worse*,
  the PR stays a draft.

**E. Decision 8's "routinely" — lead decision, proposed (derived from D's 4×).** Per cell
with `d = fine − coarse`, `min(|d|, Δ)` slides and `max(|d| − Δ, 0)` pops in one frame (at
insert where fine is higher, at removal where fine is lower). At 60 Hz a half-fade is 7.5
frames. With `U↑, C↑` the uncovered and covered sums over cells with `d > 0`, `U↓, C↓` over
`d < 0`, and `T` their total: **go only if `max(U↑ + C↑/7.5, U↓ + C↓/7.5) ≤ T/4`** for 0↔1
and for 1↔2, over the four flights' total. That is "the 4× is reachable on height alone" —
necessary, not sufficient (texture, AO and trees come on top); block heights stand in for
pixels. It needs roughly 58 % (evenly two-sided) to 87 % (one-sided) of the height covered;
the first draft's 50 % was below what the 4× needs and is withdrawn. Chosen inputs, named:
60 Hz, the four gate flights, per-cell heights as the proxy.

**F. STALE — lead decision, proposed.** The incoming node starts Δ low, so where it is
higher than the outgoing one the live frame lags the finished frame by up to 125 ms, and
`stale_pixels` counts that where the band is ≥ 2 px. Rule: report STALE on the low flight,
fade on vs `--swap-fade-ms 0`, two runs each, same binary; **if STALE kept frames with fade
on exceed both fade-off runs, the PR stays a draft and the executor stops.** Reasoning:
decision 6's "must not regress" is extended to the one gate term R6 can move the wrong way;
"never accept a PR that moves a gate metric the wrong way" (handover lessons). Note for the
report: #406's attribution files such pixels under "neither" (which it reads as far
terrain); stale pixels whose rays land inside the voxels' reach with fade on are R6's rise.

### Rejected

- Literal "batches with Removes rise" (arbitrary subset; needs the level rule in the renderer).
- Explicit swap groups in the renderer (octree knowledge in `cubara-render`).
- Fade state inside `Uploads` (the queue's #390 contract stays as it is; it only carries `sink`).
- A `write_buffer` into the origins buffer from outside the arena (S2).
- One `write_buffer` per fading node per frame (M2: hundreds to >1,000 calls a frame).
- Dithered fade (discard; the proposal's fallback only).
- Replacing the renderer's own `Instant` (FPS read-out, upload budget) with `frame_dt`.
- Changing what POPS measures (#384's decision 2).
- Halving the test's frame step to reach the 4× (that *is* lowering the bar).

### PR 2a — `feat/380a-swap-slide-render` — "feat(render): a swap can slide — lift, fades, per-node Δ (R6, #380, part 1/2)"

**2a.1 `crates/render/src/fade.rs`** (new; `mod fade;` in `lib.rs`; `pub use fade::FADE;`)

```rust
pub const FADE: Duration = Duration::from_millis(250);
pub(crate) const BIAS: f32 = 0.01;          // decision 2, blocks

/// What `advance` tells its caller about one node. One callback, because the caller's
/// arena cannot be borrowed by two closures (the reason `Sink` is one trait).
pub(crate) enum Step { Lift(f32), RiseEnded, HoldEnded, Remove }
pub(crate) enum Leave { Now, Later(f32) }

pub(crate) struct Fades { fade: Duration, nodes: BTreeMap<NodeId, NodeFade> }
struct NodeFade { lift: f32, rise_rate: f32, leave: Option<Leaving> }
struct Leaving { wait: Duration, rate: f32, floor: f32 }
```

- `new(fade)`, `set_fade`, `len`, `clear`, `forget(id)`, `is_rising(id) -> bool`.
- `arrived(&mut self, id, was_resident: bool, rise: f32) -> f32` (the lift to place at):
  1. entry exists: keep `lift`; if leaving, cancel it and set `rise_rate = leaving.rate`
     (returning id: cancels its sink, rises from where it is); if `lift == 0` drop the entry;
     return `lift`.
  2. else `was_resident` → `0.0` (an edit).
  3. else `rise > BIAS && fade > 0` → insert `{ lift: -rise, rise_rate: rise / half_secs }`,
     return `-rise`.
  4. else `0.0`.
- `leaving(&mut self, id, resident: bool, sink: f32) -> Leave`: `!resident`, `sink <= BIAS`
  or `fade == 0` → `forget(id)`, `Now`. Else take or create the entry; if not already leaving:
  `leave = Some(Leaving { wait: half, rate: sink / half_secs, floor: -sink })`,
  `lift = lift.min(-BIAS)`; return `Later(lift)`.
- `advance(&mut self, dt: Duration, mut f: impl FnMut(NodeId, Step))`, key order, per node:
  - not leaving: `lift = (lift + rise_rate * dt).min(0.0)`; emit `Lift` if changed; at 0 →
    emit `RiseEnded`, drop the entry.
  - leaving: `waited = dt.min(wait)`; `wait -= waited`;
    `lift = (lift + rise_rate * waited).min(-BIAS)` (rise continues — B);
    when `wait` reaches 0 **in this call** emit `HoldEnded` once; `sinking = dt - waited`;
    if `sinking > 0` { `rise_rate = 0`; `lift = (lift - rate * sinking).max(floor)` };
    if `lift <= floor` → emit `Remove`, drop; else emit `Lift` if changed.
  - A `dt` longer than the fade finishes everything in one call (`HoldEnded` then `Remove`).
- `Fades::advance` is decision 4's pure, clock-free function. The closed form
  `fn offset(elapsed, delta, role, fade) -> f32` and `enum Role` exist **only under
  `#[cfg(test)]`** as the oracle the tracker is checked against (one production timeline —
  Rule 5 — and no dead-code warning).

## 8. Risks and what I am not sure of

1. **The coplanar texture/AO hand-over** (§2) may keep the deciding measure under 4× on
   flat ground. Nobody has measured it; PR 1's fade-off arm gives the first number. This is
   the largest risk to "done" and why D has a pre-decided outcome.
2. **Residual above Δ** on slopes — PR 1's table; E decides.
3. **Level-0 voxel trees** cannot follow C's rule; a Δ band for one frame at ~160 m on
   every tree at the ring-0 edge. It may show in D's measure.
4. That the voxel oak and `TreeModel::Oak` are pixel-identical is read from the code (same
   structure, exact column at v = 1), not measured. If they are not, C's hand-over is as
   visible as today's, not more.
5. **Early children coplanar with a held parent** for longer than the fade — today's
   z-fight, unchanged. Fixing it needs the caller to tell the renderer a node is *held*.
6. **Replacements from an earlier batch requeued by a full arena** can land after the
   parent's Remove (the queue orders a Remove only behind its own batch). Pre-existing; the
   arena has ~8× headroom. Not fixed here.
7. **A streaming-resident parent with an empty mesh** makes a child rise with nothing above
   it (a visible 125 ms rise). Expected rare; unverified.
8. **The dirty range** can span the whole used prefix when fading slots are scattered
   (~2,000 × 16 bytes = 32 KiB in one write). Believed cheap; `fade_cost_report` measures it.
   The ~1 µs-per-`write_buffer` figure behind M2 is inferred, not measured.
9. **Tree culling boxes are not lifted**; a fading node's instances are culled by a box up
   to 4 blocks too high. Negligible; say so.
10. Whether clippy counts `self` for `too_many_arguments` is believed, not verified; the
    `allow` is harmless either way.
11. `Renderer::update` keeps its own `Instant` for the FPS read-out and upload budget. If
    review reads decision 4 as "no clock in the renderer at all", that is its own PR.
12. Three-level cascade inside 125 ms: a node capped at `−BIAS` is coplanar with its own
    held parent for a few ms (B's side effect). Documented, not fixed.
13. I did not read `trees.rs` or `scene.rs` in full; `TreeArena::insert/remove` are assumed
    to be callable at the later moments C needs (they are keyed by node id and slot).
