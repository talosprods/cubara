## 2. Findings (what the code does today)

### 2.1 What "stale" measures

- `flight.rs` `fly` (:1329 after #406): a live pass in real time at 60 Hz,
  24 blocks/s (`FLY_SPEED`), a frame kept every 0.25 s (81 in 20 s); then a
  settled pass at the same eyes.
- Settled pass (:1493–1494): `settled.far.reselect(); settled.settle(flight,
  eye)`. The reference frame is the far terrain's **converged selection at
  the exact eye** (`FarStreaming::settled`, `far_streaming.rs:359`).
- `stale_pixels` (:572): live shows the clear colour where settled shows land
  on the pixel and all four neighbours. One far ridge drawn a level coarser
  is enough to count the frame.
- The work-time terms time `Streamed::frame` (:1180), which includes
  `far.update`. They print to 0.1 ms.
- With `--frames`, `fly` starts a `FrameKeeper` thread that clones and
  PNG-encodes frames during the live pass. **A `--frames` run is loaded
  differently from a plain one and is never a run of record.**

### 2.2 How the far terrain selects

- `crates/world/src/far.rs:145` `select` / `:176` `walk`: quadtree from roots.
  A patch splits when `near` (`d < split_ratio * size`, :190) or `rough`
  (children not below the floor, :191, **and its error is known** and
  `error > max_error * d`, :193). A patch whose error is unknown is judged by
  distance alone (:142–144).
- At 1080p, High (`far_streaming.rs:61`; `far_view`, :100): `split_ratio` ≈
  2.01. Between 1,031 and 2,063 blocks a patch is **level 4** (16-block
  quads) by distance and splits to **level 3** only by roughness, when its
  p99 error exceeds `0.00194 * d` (about 3.0–3.7 blocks at 1,530–1,900). The
  floor stops further splits there. That is the band the stale pixels are
  in, and a level-4 ridge is a 16-block footprint mean (`footprint_mean`,
  :294): lower and smoother. This matches the crops. (The review re-derived
  this.)
- There is no geomorphing in `far.wgsl` and no upload queue for patches:
  `FarTerrain::insert` (`crates/render/src/far.rs:411`) is two immediate
  `write_buffer`s. **"Patch upload pacing" does not exist**; the only pacing
  is generation on the worker pool.
- The converged selection is a pure function of the view: at convergence
  every visited non-near node has a known error, so each decision is fixed
  top-down and the path the cache took does not matter.

### 2.3 Where the lag can come from

`FarStreaming::update`, `far_streaming.rs:374–453`:

1. **Reselection distance.** :384–398: the selection is recomputed when the
   eye has moved more than `RESELECT_BLOCKS = 32` (:95) since the last one,
   or the hole changed, **or any patch arrived** (:381 sets `stale`, :396
   reselects at the current eye and resets `selected_at`). So selections come
   at most 32 blocks (1.33 s, 5.3 kept frames) apart, and sooner after each
   batch of arrivals. A level-4 patch whose roughness test turns true just
   after a selection stays unsplit until the next. The issue's timestamps
   (stale at t = 3.00, 3.50, 3.75, a crop at 4.00, same ridge, no open swap)
   are consistent with one such gap. That is suggestive; it confirms nothing.
2. **Generation latency.** :413–422 request children only at the selection
   that first wants them; the parent stays drawn until all four arrive
   (`draw_set`, :184). One `far::generate` is about 85,000 `surface_height`
   calls on `cores / 2` workers (:250). The pool is one FIFO channel read
   through a shared `Mutex<Receiver>` (:254–264), so anything sent earlier
   delays a later request — for instance the burst when the hole steps,
   about every 128 blocks of x. **Latency is unmeasured**, and it includes
   that queueing.
3. **Level choice.** Ruled out at convergence (§2.2). Live and settled use
   the same `far_view`, size and quality; the hole only drops whole patches
   (`far.rs:186`).

**My reading: (1) dominates, (2) is a tail that keeps the count above 0 after
(1) is fixed, (3) is not a cause. The split between (1) and (2) is not
supported by anything measured.** Step 1 measures it.

### 2.4 Why halving `RESELECT_BLOCKS` neither decides nor fixes

Per crossing, the expected stale kept frames are roughly
(R/2 ÷ 24 blocks/s + L) ÷ 0.25 s, with L the latency. For L = 0.1 s that is
about 3.1, 1.7, 0.5 at R = 32, 16, 1; for L = 0.5 s, 4.7, 3.3, 2.1. The halved
run lands at 55–70% of base under either, so with three runs it does not
separate the hypotheses. It is run because the owner asked for it and is
reported; it does not decide. And no R reaches 0 reliably while L > 0. The
gate asks for 0, so the live selection has to **equal** the converged
exact-eye selection on every frame.

### 2.5 The ~17 px residue at ~530 blocks

Not explained by reading. One hypothesis, unverified: #406 places a pixel by
marching `WorldGen::surface_height` in 4-block steps (`surface_hit`,
`STALE_RAY_STEP`). 530 blocks is inside the voxel rings, where a coarser LOD
node draws a crest differently from full-resolution heights. A ray that
grazes a near crest in the march may, in the rendered frame, pass over it and
show the far ridge behind. Then those pixels are the same far ridge,
misplaced by the march. Step 1's "sync" run tests this: if the residue is the
far ridge, it is gone there.

## 3. Approach

**A. Reselect exactly when the selection could change.** Every decision in
`walk` compares a distance from the eye to a box with a threshold, and such a
distance changes by at most the distance the eye moves. `select` can return
its **slack**: the smallest gap between any distance it evaluated and its
threshold. Until the eye has moved that far, the selection cannot differ.
This replaces `RESELECT_BLOCKS`, makes the live selection the exact-eye one
on every frame, and costs nothing while standing still.

**B. Generate what the next selections will want before they want it** (only
if step 1 rule 4 warrants it). At a coarse cadence, select for a view "as if
everything were a margin nearer" (ahead) and "a margin farther" (behind) and
request what is missing. The results stay on the CPU; nothing is uploaded
until the exact selection asks. The picture is unchanged; only its timing.

**C. Move the decisions out of the GPU path** (`ARCHITECTURE.md` rule 6).
Today `update` needs a `Renderer`, so nothing can test the timing. A GPU-free
`FarPlan` returns what to request, remove and insert; `FarStreaming` applies
it.

Constants, all chosen, not derived:

- `PREFETCH_MARGIN`, starting value `1.0 / 16.0`: lead at distance d is
  `d·m/(1+m)`, 60 blocks (2.5 s) at 1,024. Step 1 rule 4 sizes it from the
  measured latency.
- `PREFETCH_RESELECT_BLOCKS = 8.0`.
- `RESELECT_SAFETY = 0.99`: reselect at 99% of the slack, against float
  rounding.

### Alternatives rejected

- **Just lower `RESELECT_BLOCKS`.** Cannot reach 0 (§2.4). It is a
  diagnostic.
- **Reselect on every frame the eye moved.** Exact and simpler, but one
  `select` per frame while moving, at any frame rate. **It is the fallback**
  if the slack's test (§5.4) fails for a reason you cannot fix: `exact_due =
  selected_at != Some(eye)`. Note the cost in the PR; the budget of §6.2
  still applies.
- **Draw the ahead selection.** Live would differ from settled the other way
  (a finer patch is lower in a notch), which the stale term also counts.
- **Hysteresis.** Makes the selection depend on the path, so live and settled
  disagree by design.
- **Change what STALE counts, its threshold, or the resolution.** Moves the
  gate; the owner's (§7).
- **Voxel-node prefetch.** The owner: not the lever.

- `lo`/`hi` are computed from `c.floor() as i64` instead of `c as i64`.

  and `hi + 1`, in x or z) is **not walked**: for each of those roots only
  `ground_distance(eye, box) − radius` goes into the slack. (Sound: with the
  floor, a root is in the walked range exactly when its ground distance is
  inside the radius, and its note goes to 0 as it gets there.)
- `walk` gains `slack: &mut f64` and notes `*slack = slack.min(x.abs())` for
  every eye-dependent comparison of every patch it reaches, whether or not
  short-circuiting would skip it:
  1. `ground_distance(...) - view.radius` (before the early return);
  2. `d - view.split_ratio * key.size() as f64`;
  3. if `view.min_quad > 0.0`: `d - key.quad() as f64 / 2.0 / view.min_quad`;
  4. if the error is known (`let e = error_of(key)`, called once) and
     `view.max_error` is finite and `> 0.0`: `d - e as f64 / view.max_error`.
  The split decision stays what :189–200 compute.

  error becoming known, the screen height — the caller reselects on those);
  and that **"the hole test does not depend on the eye" must be re-checked

**5.1 Harness** (PR A).

- `memo`: a per-test `RefCell<HashMap<PatchKey, PatchHeights>>` over
  `far::generate` (not a static: rule 2), shared by the modelled pool and the
  reference.
- `exact(eye, hole) -> HashSet<PatchKey>`: the converged selection — select
  against the memo's errors, generate what is missing, repeat. It repeats
  `far::build`'s loop; the precedent is the `converge` closure at :627. View:
  `far_view(eye, 1080, Some(hole), High)` with `radius: TEST_RADIUS`.
- **The pool is modelled**: `workers` workers over one FIFO in request order,
  each patch taking `service_frames` frames. `workers = 4` (the M3's `cores /
  2`). `service_frames` = step 1's measured median generation time in frames,

- **`fly(plan, eyes, hole, pool) -> Vec<HashSet<PatchKey>>`** holds `shown:

  believes.
