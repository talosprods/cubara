## Working rules that held up today

- **Issue first, with binding decisions.**
  - Subagent in a worktree with its own `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`; it pushes a branch and opens no PR.
  - The lead reviews the diff and re-measures on a quiet machine (3× alternating against main).
  - Then the lead opens the PR with `--auto --squash`.
- **A second-model code review before merging big work pays off.** On #379 it found a blocker (far heights absolute while `anchor.y` was 4,096) that the gate A/B confirmed: a fake 30% "speedup" at the flight eye.
- **Lessons from the 2026-11-10 critical review** (`fable-review-2026-11-10.md`, on the Linux laptop; the findings are summarised in issues #384–#394):
  - split a PR over ~1,200 lines or three crates;
  - every render or perf issue carries a CPU/frame budget beside the GPU one;
  - an invented threshold is labelled "chosen, not derived";
  - a diagnosis that flips cites the measurement that flipped it;
  - never accept a PR that moves a gate metric the wrong way with "not confirmed".
- **Gate item 0 is judged at the bench's default size (1080p).** Flight rows are recorded at that size from now on. HOLES depends on resolution (the Mac session's finding).
- **Measurements on a loaded machine are not measurements.** The swap-lag numbers taken under load 10–13 were wrong by 4×.
- **Never use a `pgrep -f` wait loop.** It matches itself and never ends.
